## How to sign

Choose one:

1. **GitHub (recommended).** When the CLA check comments on your pull request,
   reply with exactly:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

   Your GitHub username, account id, the date and a link to the comment are
   recorded publicly on the repository's `cla-signatures` branch.

2. **Email.** Send to the Maintainer (address in `pyproject.toml`) the
   statement *"I agree to the HVX Individual Contributor License Agreement version
   1.0"*, together with your full name, GitHub username, the email address(es) of
   your commits, and whether you are employed or contracted by an organisation that
   may have rights in your work. The Maintainer then records your GitHub account as
   signed; the email is kept privately.

3. **Signed document.** Ask for a PDF copy to sign by hand or with a qualified
   electronic signature, for example where your employer requires it.

Contributing on behalf of a company? Ask in your pull request for the Corporate
Contributor License Agreement.
